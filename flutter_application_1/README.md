Each member has one or two specified roles. But, there will be some fluidity between the different roles. Most members will probably do tasks that fall under the responsablities of a roll that isn't their own. This is okay and prefered!

Each member is responsible for their own work and meeting any given deadline. Each member must communicate if they are: 
- Struggling to meet expectations
- Don't have enough time to finish by a given deadline
- Need assistence on any portion of the project
- Wanting to make a commit to the main branch of the project
- Any other issues or concerns about the project.


We will have meetings based on the necessity and difficulty of the problem. Our normal meeting time will be either Tuesday from 11:00am - 12:00pm or Thursday 11:00am - 12:00pm or 12:00pm - 1:00pm, based on availablity of that given week. Each member is expected to show up to all meetings on time. If a group member isn't available, they must message the group at somepoint before the time of the scheduled meeting.

If a deadline or problem is relatively small, or progress is going smoothly, the group can decide to opt out (unanimously) of the weekly meeting. If this is chosen, the communication through teams must increase somewhat to counteract the lack of meeting. If progress slips because of this case above, Drew Williams, the Task manager, must prove this with examples of behavior, and request the team reinstate the weekly meeting. Futhermore, if any additional meetings are required to make up for lack of progress, no matter if the group can't meet fully, each group member must meet in person with another group member to work on the project.

- Proceedures

During general team meetings, each member will talk about the current progress and ideas they are working on. Each member must be detailed in their description of progress done and provide future steps they will take this week to ensure further progress.

Each group member has specific things they will be tasked with. Each member must do their own assigned tasks. Help to other members of the group and the swapping/changing of roles is welcomes, as long as each member has significant contributions to the project.

Any issues or ideas, must be voted on unanimously to move forward. In the case of a continued issue and disagreement, we will ask Dr. Goadrich to mediate during one of our meetings.

- Participation

During our weekly meetings we will set time goals, and keep each other accountable if that goal isn't reached.

If a member misses a meeting, they must let us know in advance. During this case, we will provide a summary of what took place during the meeting, to ensure that no one is left out of important discussions.

If a member doesn't seem to be participating to a favorable degree, as a group we will try to discuss the issues and try to resolve them. If group mediation isn't successful, then we will bring up the issues to Dr. Goadrich.

Each member is also responsable with logging their hours on our group time sheet.

Personal Accountability

Everyone is to make it to all of the meetings if at all possible, and let the group know in advance if they aren't going to make it.

We should strive to have as good communication as possible in order to make the best project we can.

Everyone will participate actively in this project and do our best to keep up with the work load.

- Breach of Contract

If a group member misses a meeting without notice, we will have a short talk about communication and ask for improved communication.

If a group member doesn't make the hour quota without communication, then we will ask for increased time the next week.

If a group member's work isn't satesfactory, we will try our best to aid that group member or refer them to Dr. Goadrich.

If any group member doesn't hold their weight, we will try out best to come to an agreement within the group.

If any member is deemed by the majority of the group as a hinderence, and no other action the group can think of would help, we will bring it up to Dr. Goadrich and request you be removed from the project.
