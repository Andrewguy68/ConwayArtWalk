- Procedures

During general team meetings, each member will talk about the current progress and ideas they are working on. Each member must be detailed in their description of progress done and provide future steps they will take this week to ensure further progress.

Each group member has specific things they will be tasked with. Each member must do their own assigned tasks. Help to other members of the group and the swapping/changing of roles is welcomes, as long as each member has significant contributions to the project.

Any issues or ideas, must be voted on unanimously to move forward. In the case of a continued issue and disagreement, we will ask Dr. Goadrich to mediate during one of our meetings.

- Participation

During our weekly meetings we will set time goals, and keep each other accountable if that goal isn't reached.

If a member misses a meeting, they must let us know in advance. During this case, we will provide a summary of what took place during the meeting, to ensure that no one is left out of important discussions.

If a member doesn't seem to be participating to a favorable degree, as a group we will try to discuss the issues and try to resolve them. If group mediation isn't successful, then we will bring up the issues to Dr. Goadrich.

Each member is also responsable with logging their hours on our group time sheet.
